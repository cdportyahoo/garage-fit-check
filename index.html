How to use the planner

Enter your garage and vehicle measurements. Drag the car or a cabinet to move it. Select a cabinet and drag its white corner handle to resize. Use the fields below for exact measurements. On iPhone, open directly in Safari.

Save layout replaces the one saved layout in this browser on this device. Tap Load saved layout to restore it. Reset restores the example layout and does not erase your saved layout. Clearing browser data can erase it.

Checking…

Purple/dark blocks = cabinets. Slate = car. Red = overlap or outside the garage. Dragging snaps to the nearest inch. FIT means the simplified layout meets your desired vehicle gap; REVISE means that gap is too small; STOP means an overlap or an object outside the garage.
