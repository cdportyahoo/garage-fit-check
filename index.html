How to use the planner
  1. Measure the usable inside width and depth of your garage, in inches.
  2. Choose a vehicle example, then enter your actual vehicle measurements, including mirrors.
  3. Tap a cabinet number or Steps button. Use Move to place it or Resize to change its footprint.
  4. Review the warnings, check door opening and access in your real garage, then save your layout and PDF.

On iPhone, open directly in Safari. Scroll outside the drawing while arranging your layout.
