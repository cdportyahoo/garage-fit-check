STORAGE COMPARISON

18 or 24 inches deep?
Keep room for the car.

A deeper cabinet takes six more inches from the space beside your vehicle. Start with your available clearance, then choose the storage.

Specifications checked November 11, 2026. This is a comparison of published dimensions, not a hands-on product review. Manufacturer links below are ordinary links; this page contains no affiliate links.

The quick choice

Consider 18-inch depth when the side gap is tight. It preserves six inches compared with a 24-inch cabinet of the same width. Check that the items you want to store fit the cabinet's usable interior.

Consider 24-inch depth when you have room to spare. It offers a deeper outside footprint for larger items, but check internal dimensions, shelves and door opening before deciding. Outside depth alone does not tell you usable storage capacity.

Before choosing either cabinet

  • Measure what you want to store and confirm usable interior dimensions.
  • Check the exact product's shelf limits and installation instructions.
  • Mark the full footprint on your floor, including offsets and projections.
  • Park all vehicles and open the doors, drawers and hatch you use.
  • Compare the delivered cost, rather than just the cabinet's advertised price.
