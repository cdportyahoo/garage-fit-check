## Current recommendation

For the website-only, low-maintenance model, prioritize verification of EasyGarage's current enrollment/Arkansas eligibility and FLEXIMOUNTS' actual rate and terms. Home Depot Creator merits consideration only if a website without an established creator following is eligible and affiliate-link participation does not require content campaigns. Home Depot standard is the clearest observed website route but its 1%/one-day economics are weaker. Hold Lowe's until its data/AI restrictions, actual storage rate and website readiness are addressed. This ranking is a practical assessment, not approval or a guarantee of earnings.

No program's compatibility with all competitors has been fully confirmed. Do not accept a contract on the assumption of non-exclusivity. Compare the actual agreement, prohibited promotion methods, exclusions, returns/reversals, fees, payout minimums and content rights before committing.
