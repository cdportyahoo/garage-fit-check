GOLF CART GARAGE PLANNING

Can a car and golf cart fit in your garage?

They may fit side by side—even with storage. Start with the usable width, then check the door opening, parking route, and space to get in and out.

Measure the cart you actually park

Measure overall width and length with the accessories you intend to leave attached: mirrors, golf bags, rear seats, baskets, and other projections. Check the roof height separately. A lifted cart or longer passenger model can need a different space from a compact two-seater.

For context, Club Car lists its Onward 2 Passenger at 48.75 inches wide, 91.5 inches long, and 71 inches high with a top. E-Z-GO lists the 2025 Freedom RXV ELiTE Lithium at 47 inches wide, 94.5 inches long, and 69.5 inches high with a roof. These are model-specific examples, not dimensions for every cart. The 24- and 30-inch gaps are chosen example amounts, not clearance standards or a promise of comfortable access. Check your doors, passengers, and belongings in the real garage.

In a 216-inch-wide garage, the same 224-inch budget is 8 inches short. Changing the cabinet from 18 to 24 inches deep would use 6 more inches. Try a different storage wall or parking position rather than assuming a “two-car garage” has enough room.

Check depth, height, and the way in

Depth: include anything in the parking path

A 195-inch-long vehicle in a 264-inch-deep garage leaves 69 inches to distribute in front and behind. An 18-inch front-wall cabinet in that same path reduces the remainder to 51 inches. Golf bags extending behind the cart belong in its measured length too.

Height: measure the lowest obstruction

Compare the cart’s actual overall height with the clear garage-door opening and anything lower along its path. Check the opened door, tracks, opener, shelving, and the cart’s canopy or accessories. The planner is a top view; it does not test overhead clearance.

Access: a parked fit is only part of the job

Measure the clear door opening between the tracks and jambs. Check whether you can bring each vehicle in and out without moving the other. A shared opening may require parking one at a time. A footprint calculation does not establish turning space or confirm that a driver can get out.

Before you buy storage

Mark the proposed cabinet footprint with removable tape suitable for your floor. Park both vehicles, load the golf bags, open the doors and drawers you use, and walk the route to the house. Repeat the check with each vehicle a little away from its ideal parking position.

For electric carts, follow the instructions for your exact cart, battery, and charger when choosing a charging location. A space-saving drawing does not verify charging suitability.
